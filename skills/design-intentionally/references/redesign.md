# Redesign contract

Audit before editing:

- route structure and navigation labels;
- real copy, legal text, forms, field names, and interaction outcomes;
- brand assets, color and typography tokens, and component system;
- representative desktop and mobile renders;
- loading, empty, error, disabled, and focused states that exist today.

In `preserve` mode, modernize hierarchy, spacing, typography, responsive behavior, imagery, and motion without silently changing preserved behavior or content.

In `overhaul` mode, larger visual changes are allowed, but every item in the intent's `preserve` list remains fixed. Document deliberate information-architecture or copy changes separately.

Do not treat a visual redesign as authorization to replace the framework, rewrite unrelated business logic, change routes, or install a new component system. Verify existing and changed flows after each coherent boundary.
